# M2 Task 9: `okf::analysis::sprawl` (2026-10-27T16:00:18Z)

Issue #11, Task 9 of `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-27-okf-mcp-m2-analysis.md`. Ports
`okf.py`'s `VOLATILE_RE`, `DATE_RE`, `LEADIN_RE`, `SPRAWL_*`, `volatile_reason`,
`sprawl_of` and `cmd_sprawl` (1236-1337) over the snapshot, so `sprawl` gives the same
JSON as `okf.py sprawl --json` (top `-n`, or one named concept).

## Added

- `src/okf/analysis/sprawl.rs`:
  - `sprawl_of(&SnapshotDoc) -> SprawlReport`: the score (lines/20 + sections/2 +
    sources/3 + 1.5 per volatile heading or lead-in + distinct dates/5, rounded to one
    decimal), every section with its size, volatile headings and bold lead-ins, distinct
    dates outside fenced code, split candidates, and the exempt type (`Backlog`).
  - `sprawl(&Snapshot, SprawlQuery) -> Result<Vec<SprawlReport>, OkfError>`: the named
    concept (exempt or not), or the `n` highest non-exempt scores, ties by path.
    Concepts are scored in parallel with `rayon`; exempt rows are dropped before the
    sort, and the top `n` is partitioned with `select_nth_unstable_by`.
  - `SprawlQuery` (`Default` is `okf.py`'s `-n 5`), `SprawlSection`, `VolatileItem`
    (keys in `okf.py`'s order) and `SPRAWL_WARN` (16) for Task 10's lint.
  - The regexes emulate Python's `\b` with consumed non-word neighbours and widen `i`
    (and `[a-z]`) with U+0130/U+0131 for `re.I`; `dates_in` resumes at each date's end
    as `findall` does.
  - `py_float_sum`: Python 3.12+'s compensated `sum()` of floats (see Notes).
- `src/okf/markdown.rs`: `pub(crate) fence_marker` and `pub(crate) next_fence`, now
  used by both `headings` and sprawl's code-line set, so the fence rule has one
  definition.
- Tests: 6 in `analysis::sprawl::tests`: the plan's three, plus
  `scores_add_like_python_sum`, `dates_resume_after_each_match_like_findall` and
  `quirks_match_okf_py`.

## Notes

- **Python's `sum` is compensated.** Since 3.12, `sum()` of floats uses Neumaier
  summation, so it can differ from a `+` chain in the last bit, and that bit can flip
  the one-decimal rounding. On `pg_knowledge_catalog`, `/home/robot-mower.md` scored
  3.1 with the plan's `+` chain and 3.2 in `okf.py`; with `py_float_sum` it matches.
- **Oracle checks.** Every expectation was produced by `okf.py` (Python 3.14.4, sha256
  `b528dd51...`) on the same files. The JSON equals `okf.py sprawl --json -n 100000`
  (compared as values: `json.dumps` escapes non-ASCII, serde_json writes it raw) on the
  test catalogs, `tests/fixtures/catalog` (36 rows), `pg_knowledge_catalog` (142 rows,
  plus three concepts by name including the exempt `projects/things-to-fix`), and 400
  generated files mixing volatile words, Python-cased letters, dates, fences, lead-ins
  and broken frontmatter (235 rows).
- No divergences from `okf.py` are known.
