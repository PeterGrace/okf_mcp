# M5 Task 13: end-to-end and concurrency tests

Issue #102. The write path over real HTTP with rmcp clients, as an agent sees it.

## Added

- `tests/writes.rs`, six tests:
  - `create_link_index_log_publish_and_export`: `create_concept`, `index` and `log`,
    seen through the writer's overlay and not by a reader, then `publish`, `history`
    and `GET /catalogs/pg/export`;
  - `concurrent_log_publishes_from_two_keys_keep_both_bullets`: two keys publish
    `log.md` at once; exactly one publish replays, and both bullets are kept;
  - `an_agent_sees_a_conflict_and_can_redo_its_edit`: a `Conflict` tool result,
    `pending_changes` showing the entry `stale`, then `discard` and a redo;
  - `a_changeset_survives_a_server_restart`: the unlabelled `default` changeset is
    found again by a new client of a new server on the same database;
  - `x_okf_changeset_keeps_two_changesets_of_one_key_apart`: two labels, two
    changesets, one published without the other;
  - `concurrent_writes_from_two_connections_of_one_key_share_one_changeset`: two
    unlabelled connections write at once into the shared `default` changeset and no
    edit is lost.
- `TestServer::client_with(key, headers)` and `TestServer::restart(&mut self, config)`
  in `tests/support/server.rs`.

## Changed

- `TestServer::client` is a wrapper over `client_with`; `start` and `restart` share a
  private `serve_app`. Every M4 and Task 6 caller is unchanged.
- The plan's Decision 6 and Task 13, and the design's "Write" step 5, record the Task 8
  decision below.

## Notes

- **Overlay rebuilt under the row lock: kept.** Measured with a throwaway test (not
  committed) on the real catalog (193 documents), debug build: about 25 ms per
  sequential `log` write, against about 6.6 ms for a `pending_changes` round trip that
  builds no overlay; eight connections of one key writing to one changeset at once all
  finished within about 190 ms. Writes to one changeset are serialized by the row lock
  anyway, so reusing the overlay would only shorten each turn, and keeping a cached
  overlay in step with each write's entries is the incremental work Decision 6
  rejected.
- The restart test uses no label: it relies on `default` being stable across MCP
  sessions (Decision 1), not on `X-OKF-Changeset` as the plan's Step 3 said.
- The concurrency tests send their calls with `tokio::join!`, so they exercise the
  publish lock and replay, and the changeset row lock.
- `cargo test --test writes`: 6 passed in about 2.4 s (each test starts one Postgres
  container).
