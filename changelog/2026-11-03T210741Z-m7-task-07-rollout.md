## Found while rolling out

- **Secret key names:** an object-store secret named `SECRET_ACCESS_KEY` (AWS style) is
  what people write. The first sync asked for `ACCESS_SECRET_KEY`, and the failing
  `ExternalSecret` in wave `-1` held back every later wave.
- **The region:** barman-cloud must be given the store's region. Without it, the store
  answered `400 Bad Request` and archiving failed.
- **The Service's IP family:** the cluster forces Services to `RequireDualStack`, so
  setting `PreferDualStack` kept the app out of sync. The field is now left out.
- **Plugin flags:** `kubectl -n NS cnpg ...` fails; flags go after the plugin's name.
