# M12 Task 6: Mail through a `Mailer` and the apalis `SendMail` job

## Added
- `mail::Mailer` (an `#[async_trait]` trait object) with three providers: SMTP (lettre over rustls), Postmark (`POST /email` over reqwest) and `log`. `[mail]` in the config file chooses one; SMTP passwords and Postmark tokens come from the environment variables it names, read when the server starts.
- `mail::templates`: `setup`, `reset`, `method_added` and `test`, each plain text with an HTML twin; `mail::link` builds emailed links from `public_url`.
- `mail::queue`: `enqueue` (renders, then queues a `SendMail` job in `apalis.jobs`), the worker (`spawn_worker`, run by `serve` unless `[mail] worker = false`), and `list`, `retry`, `delete` and `prune` over the queue.

## Changed
- `App` holds the mailer (`App::mailer`) and lends its clock as a handle (`App::clock_handle`). `App::load` fails when a mail secret is missing, so `serve` stops at start-up; commands that only enqueue never read the secrets.
- `http::serve` runs the mail worker and waits for it on shutdown.
- The hourly prune deletes sent mail jobs after 7 days and failed ones after 30.
- Test servers turn the mail worker off, and the test database pool has 8 connections.

## Notes
- Retries are durable: a retryable failure moves the job's `run_at` (30 s, doubling, capped at 1 h) and the job is fetched again once due, for 24 hours from when it was queued. A permanent failure (an SMTP 5xx, a Postmark 4xx other than 401 or 429, a malformed recipient) fails the job at once. A manual retry starts a new 24-hour window. All of it is tested under real apalis on the test database, with two workers on one queue sending each mail exactly once.
- A worker that stops on a backend error restarts after 30 s under `<name>-<restarts>`: apalis-postgres re-registers an existing worker row only under a session-scoped advisory lock another pooled connection may hold.
- The queue listing never shows a message body; job payloads hold the rendered mail, setup and reset links included, until they are pruned. `Mail`, `SendMail` and `Template` have hand-written `Debug` that leaves bodies and links out; only the `log` provider writes a body to the log, by design.
