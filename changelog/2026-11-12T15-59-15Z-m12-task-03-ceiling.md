# M12 Task 3: the ceiling and capped self-issued credentials

## Added
- `store::ceiling`: `ceilings`, `ceiling_of`, `MemberAccess`,
  `set_member_access`, `entitle`, `unentitle`, `entitlements`,
  `may_set_member_access` and the pure `cap`.
- `Keys::ceiling_of(user_id)`.
- A test that every write that can move a ceiling (joining or leaving a
  team, creating, re-owning or deleting a catalog, member access,
  entitlements and deleting a user) changes `ceilings()` and bumps
  `admin_generation`.

## Changed
- The key cache loads every ceiling with the keys and caps each
  self-issued key and grant at the lower of its own level and the ceiling.
  Admin-issued credentials are unchanged.
- `KeyCache::from_records` takes the ceilings as a second argument.
- Member access and entitlement changes bump `admin_generation`, so a
  lowered ceiling lowers self-issued credentials within one poll.

## Notes
- `entitle` refuses `admin` (409); unknown users are 404 `UnknownName`,
  unknown catalogs 404 `CatalogNotSelected`.
- The M11 writes that change a ceiling's other inputs already bumped
  `admin_generation` (`teams::set_member`, `remove_member`,
  `catalogs::create`, `set_owner`, `delete`, `users::delete`); entitlements
  cascade with their catalog and their user. No other code writes
  `team_members`, `catalogs.owner_team_id`, `catalogs.member_access` or
  `user_catalogs`.
