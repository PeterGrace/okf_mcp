Every outward step ran with pgrace's go-ahead, given in the session.

## Done

- **Image (go-ahead 1):** tagged `v0.8.0` on `0ee3857` (`main` after Tasks 1 to 5) and pushed
  the tag. The `image` workflow published `ghcr.io/petergrace/okf-mcp:0.8.0` and its
  `:sha-<short>` tag. Its separate run on `main` was skipped: the tag run publishes both
  tags. The image pulled through the cluster's registry cache and reported
  `okf-mcp 0.8.0`.
- **Backup:** before the deploy, an on-demand plugin backup of the database completed in
  8 s. 0.8.0 is forward-only, because migration 0004 drops `changesets.label`. Going back
  to 0.7.0 means restoring from that backup (`docs/operations.md`, section 8).
- **Deployment (go-ahead 2):** the site's Argo CD repository changed only the Deployment's
  image tag, from 0.7.0 to 0.8.0. The app became Synced/Healthy, and the new pod was
  ready about 7 minutes after the tag.
