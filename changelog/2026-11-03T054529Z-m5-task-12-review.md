# M5 Task 12: review of PR #110

## Changed

- `store::changesets::stranded` takes `key_id: Option<&str>` and `catalog_id: Option<i64>` and
  filters in SQL (`None` turns a filter off). `stranded_sessions` passes its key unless the
  caller is admin, and the `catalog` argument's id, so a non-admin call never loads other keys'
  paths. Reach and the calling connection's own changeset are still filtered in Rust.
- `adopt_changeset` reads the changeset unlocked only for its catalog id. After
  `lock_catalog` it re-reads it with `changesets::lock` (`FOR UPDATE`, from Task 8) and runs the
  open, access and ownership checks on that row. A changeset closed by a concurrent publish,
  discard or prune now gets the uniform "no open changeset N in a catalog this key reaches".
  A concurrent adoption is either seen or waits. The earlier `version` check, and its `Busy`
  answer, are gone.
- Merged `origin/main` (Tasks 4, 7 and 8): `OkfServer::tools` keeps `writes_router` and
  `lifecycle_router`, and `src/app.rs` imports both `Overlays` and `changesets`.
